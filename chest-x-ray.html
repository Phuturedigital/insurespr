Chest X-ray in Randburg

A chest X-ray produces images of the chest, including the lungs, heart region, airways and chest wall. It supports a referring healthcare professional’s assessment; the website does not select the test or interpret the result.

Choose the correct route: this page is for a diagnostic chest X-ray request. Visa chest X-rays and employer-led occupational chest X-rays have separate requirements. Tell the clinical team privately if you are or may be pregnant.

The right chest-imaging route

The reason for the examination determines the next step.

  1. Request

    Provide the written clinical request and choose diagnostic chest X-ray.

  2. Confirm

    Staff confirm scope, documents, pregnancy check and appointment status.

  3. Image

    Attend for the confirmed examination and approved reporting route.

Chest X-ray: practical answers

Diagnostic, visa and occupational routes are kept separate so the requirements stay clear.

What is this service?
A diagnostic X-ray examination that creates images of structures inside the chest for professional interpretation.
Who is it for?
People with a written request from an authorised healthcare professional for a diagnostic chest X-ray supported by the practice.
What is outside this route?
This is not the visa chest X-ray page, an employer medical-surveillance programme or a stand-alone TB diagnosis. It is not an emergency service. Seek appropriate urgent care for severe breathing difficulty, chest pain or another emergency concern.
How much does it cost?
Practice confirmation required. No public cash or self-pay price has been approved.
Do I need a referral or request?
Expect to provide a written, signed clinical request. InsureSPR must confirm that the requester, indication and examination are within its supported scope.
Is medical aid accepted or is it self-pay?
Practice confirmation required. Medical-aid claims, authorisation and self-pay arrangements have not been approved for publication.
Appointment or walk-in?
This is a request-based route. A submitted date is only a preference until InsureSPR confirms the attendance arrangement.
What should I bring or disclose?
Bring the original request and identification the practice confirms. Tell the clinical team privately before imaging if you are or may be pregnant. Do not place symptoms, pregnancy or identity numbers in the public booking notes.
How long does it take?
Practice confirmation required. No examination or total-visit duration has been approved for publication.
How and when are results provided?
Practice confirmation required. A professional must interpret the images; the approved report recipient and turnaround time have not been published.
Where is it and what should I do next?
InsureSPR is at 7 Malibongwe Drive, EmedCentre, Randburg. Submit the diagnostic chest X-ray request, then wait for the examination and document requirements to be confirmed.
Authoritative background: read the SAHPRA guidance on requests for medical X-rays, ACR/RSNA chest X-ray overview and IAEA pregnancy and radiology guidance. These sources describe general practice, not an InsureSPR diagnosis, price or turnaround promise.
