See progress that weight alone cannot show

A DXA body-composition scan measures lean mass, body fat and bone density. For gym-goers and runners, that creates a more useful baseline for tracking changes in training, nutrition and recovery than weight or BMI alone.

You can also request a focused bone-density scan. InsureSPR will confirm the right scan, price, preparation, eligibility and appointment time before you attend.
