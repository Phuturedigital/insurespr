Request a booking

See progress that weight alone cannot show

A DXA body-composition scan measures lean mass, body fat and bone density. For gym-goers and runners, that creates a more useful baseline for tracking changes in training, nutrition and recovery than weight or BMI alone.

You can also request a focused bone-density scan. InsureSPR will confirm the right scan, price, preparation, eligibility and appointment time before you attend.

Body-composition scan

See total body-fat percentage, lean mass and bone mass. Repeating the same measurement over time can help you evaluate changes alongside your training or nutrition plan.

Before booking: public price, preparation, eligibility and report timing still require practice approval.

Request a body scan

Bone-density scan

Measure how dense your bones are. This is a separate clinical question from sports performance and may be requested to assess bone health or osteoporosis risk.

Before booking: public price, preparation, referral requirements and report timing still require practice approval.
