Strong. Prevent. Reclaim.

SPR is the thinking that connects a useful measurement to a practical next step. First establish a baseline. Then understand it, act where appropriate and review change over time.

See how the flow worksChoose a service

One loop, not three disconnected promises

The exact clinical or practical step depends on the service and the person. SPR provides the sequence; it does not pretend one scan or pathway is right for everyone.

  1. 01Measure

    Use the appropriate imaging, body-and-bone measurement or structured medical route.

  2. 02Understand

    Put the result in context: what it shows, what it does not show and what needs professional interpretation.

  3. 03Act

    Choose the relevant next step—clinical follow-up, a training or nutrition conversation, or a workplace process.

  4. 04Review

    Where repeat measurement is appropriate, compare like with like and use change to guide the next decision.

S · Strong

Start from something useful

Strong is about a credible starting point. For an active person, body-composition DXA can measure lean mass, body fat and bone mass beyond weight or BMI alone. For an imaging need, the starting point may be an X-Ray route. The measure should match the question.

P · Prevent

Use evidence before guesswork

Prevent means recognising useful information earlier and choosing an informed response. It is not a guarantee that illness or injury can be prevented, and it is not a diagnosis made by a webpage.

R · Reclaim

Make progress visible

Reclaim is the review step: understand what changed, keep what is working and adjust what is not. It supports a clearer conversation about progress without reducing health to one number.
