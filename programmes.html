Programmes

Everything on offer, in words you already use

The practice’s own three programmes and its full service list. Each keeps its proper clinical name, gains a sentence explaining what it actually is, and a line on what you get out of it.

InsureStrong

Build and maintain healthy bone and muscle mass

Who it’s for

Anyone who wants to know their starting point and build on it — including people who train and want real numbers rather than a bathroom scale.

What’s included

  • DXA bone density
  • DXA body composition
  • Fitness & nutrition guidance
  • Repeat scan
You cannot build on a number you do not have. This programme starts with the scan, because everything after it is guesswork otherwise.

InsurePrevent

Stop bone and muscle loss before it starts

Who it’s for

People who want their fracture risk assessed properly — posture, mobility and bone strength together — before a stumble becomes a break.

What’s included

  • Nurse-led osteoporosis clinic
  • Posture, flexibility & balance
  • Bone density scanning
  • Ongoing management
One consultation, one price. The practice runs this as a single appointment with the bone density scans and assessments included — its own stated approach, rather than three separate bookings.

InsureReclaim

Restore what has been lost, so you can live boldly

Who it’s for

People rebuilding after a fall, a fracture, or a long stretch of doing less — where the goal is getting specific things back.

What’s included

  • Personal plan
  • Monthly checkpoints
  • Targeted nutrition
  • Corrective exercise
  • Health coaching
The goals are yours to set. Carrying your own shopping, getting off the floor unaided, walking without watching every step — measured monthly so drift gets caught early.

The full list

Everything else the practice does

These are the practice’s own service names, kept exactly as it writes them. Underneath each one: what it actually is, then what it gets you.

DXA bone density screening

A ten-minute scan measuring how strong your bones are, so thinning gets caught while there is still time to act on it.

You leave knowing whether to act now or re-check in a few years — instead of finding out from a fracture.

DXA body composition

The same scan, showing how much muscle, fat and bone you carry and where. What you are made of, not just what you weigh.

A measurement that moves when the work is working. A scale can sit still for months while your body changes underneath it.

Osteoporosis clinic consultation

Nurse-led care if your bones have already thinned — the same person following your case, rather than starting again with a stranger each visit.

One appointment at one price with the scans included, covering primary and secondary osteoporosis.

Posture, flexibility and balance assessment

A proper test of how likely you are to fall — and which specific thing to work on to change that answer.

A straight answer on your fall risk, and the one or two things that actually shift it, rather than being told to “be careful”.

Neck and shoulder mobility

Getting range of movement back in the joints that stiffen first and change how everything below them works.

Top shelves, blind spots, and a night’s sleep your shoulder does not interrupt.

Back, hip and knee mobility

The joints that decide whether you can get out of a chair, climb stairs and walk properly without thinking about it.

Stairs and chairs go back to being things you do, not things you plan around.

Breatheez — diaphragmatic gateway

Learning to breathe with your diaphragm instead of your neck and shoulders. Shallow breathing keeps those muscles permanently switched on, which is where a lot of neck and upper back pain quietly comes from.

The tension that always comes back stops coming back, because the load moves off muscles that were never meant to carry it.

Overuse injury correction

Sorting out the aches that come from doing the same movement too often — and fixing the pattern causing them, not just the sore spot.

The ache stops returning, because the movement that caused it is not the movement you leave with.

Health coaching

Someone in your corner between appointments, for the long stretch where a plan either becomes a habit or quietly stops.

The plan survives a normal week, because somebody notices it slipping before it stops.

Sports and fitness assessment

Real body composition for people who train — sharper programming, better nutrition decisions, fewer injuries from imbalances nobody spotted.

Training decisions made on your own limb-by-limb breakdown, with left-right imbalances caught while they are still a number rather than an injury.

Working together

If you refer patients or run a facility

The practice lists three audiences, and two of them are not the patient.

Medical facilities and health professionals

Screening, diagnosis and ongoing management of primary and secondary osteoporosis, with a nurse-led clinic behind it. Your patient comes back understanding their own result, which makes your next conversation with them a shorter one.

Sports and fitness facilities

Body composition analysis for performance and injury prevention, available to your members. Objective numbers to programme against, and a clear before-and-after when the work pays off.

Talk about referrals

Not sure which one you need?

Most people do not, and that is fine — it is what the first consultation is for. Almost everybody starts with the same scan anyway.
