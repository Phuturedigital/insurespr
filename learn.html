Plain English

Every word you might have to look up

If a term appears anywhere on this site or the practice's own, it is explained here.

Osteoporosis

Bones thinned enough that they break easily. Usually causes no symptoms until something breaks.

Osteopenia

Bones thinner than they should be, but not yet osteoporosis. The stage where you can still change the direction.

Sarcopenia

The muscle loss that creeps in with age. It is why standing up out of a low chair gets harder.

Bone mineral density (BMD)

How much mineral is packed into your bone. More mineral, harder to break.

T-score

One number comparing your bones to a healthy young adult's. The lower it goes below zero, the thinner your bones.

Body composition

What you are made of — muscle, fat and bone, and where each sits — rather than what you weigh.

Diaphragmatic breathing

Breathing with the big muscle under your ribs instead of your neck and shoulders. What Breatheez trains.

Healthspan

The years you stay well, as opposed to the years you stay alive. The gap between the two is the thing worth shrinking.

Fragility fracture

A break from something that should not have broken a bone — a trip on level ground, a fall from standing height.

Overuse injury

Damage from repeating one movement too often, rather than from a single accident.
